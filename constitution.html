Liga ng mga Gwapings Constitution

Updated: 11/24/2026

Preamble

This document serves as the official Constitution (“the Constitution”) for the keeper league Liga ng mga Gwapings hosted on sports.ws.

Article I. Definitions

For the purposes of this Constitution, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

  • League – The organized competition governed by this Constitution.
  • Constitution – This document and any amendments officially adopted by the Commissioner or League governance.
  • Owner – The individual responsible for managing a team within the League.
  • Team – A franchise, squad, or unit controlled by an Owner and participating in the League.
  • Commissioner – The individual with ultimate authority to interpret, enforce, and amend the rules of the League.

Article II. Policies

Governing Rules

This section defines the hierarchy of rules in the league and establishes the authority of the Commissioner. It clarifies which rules take precedence, the scope of the Commissioner’s powers, and the finality of decisions.

  • Default Platform Rules: The league operates under the default keeper rules of sports.ws, except as modified herein.
  • Superseding Authority: This document supersedes platform rules where conflicts exist.
  • Commissioner Authority: The Commissioner retains absolute authority to interpret, amend, or override any rule or decision.
  • Finality of Actions: Any action or inaction by the Commissioner is final and binding.

League Membership

  • Team Approval: All team memberships require prior approval from the Commissioner. No team may participate without formal acceptance.
  • Team Exit Notification: Any team planning to exit the league must inform the Commissioner prior to the start of the next season’s draft. Failure to notify in a timely manner may result in forfeiture of rights or penalties as determined by the Commissioner.
  • New Team Limits & League Cap: A maximum of four (4) new teams may join or be created per season. Under no circumstances may the total number of teams in the league exceed the maximum league size of thirty (30) teams, unless explicitly waived by the Commissioner.
  • Replacement Priority: Priority for new team membership is given to prospective owners replacing exiting teams. Returning or new ownership taking over existing rosters shall take precedence over the creation of entirely new expansion teams.

Owner Conduct and Participation

  • League Purpose: The League is intended to be a fun, competitive environment for passionate Owners. All participants are expected to foster a positive experience, treat fellow participants respectfully, and promote fair gameplay.
  • Forum and Communication Conduct: Participation in forums, chats, or other League-related platforms is encouraged. Trash talk is permitted, but harassment, collusion, or verbally targeting another Owner is strictly prohibited.
  • Reporting Violations: Owners are encouraged to submit screenshots of forum posts, social media interactions, or direct messages that constitute harassment. The Commissioner shall determine if an Owner has crossed the line.
  • Disciplinary Action: The Commissioner may issue warnings or expulsion for inappropriate behavior. Warnings issued to an Owner may be notified to the League.
  • Activity Expectations: Owners are expected to maintain a reasonable level of participation. Daily activity is encouraged but not mandatory.
  • Monitoring and Warnings: The Commissioner will monitor gameplay and participation. Patterns of inactivity or obstructive behavior may result in warnings. Multiple warnings may lead to removal from the League at the Commissioner’s discretion.
  • Trade Responsiveness: Owners must respond to trade offers in a reasonable timeframe. Ignoring trades or delaying responses unreasonably is discouraged to maintain fairness and engagement.

Article III. League Structure, Divisions & Movement

Section 3.1 — Competitive Pyramid & Conference Alignment

  • League Size & Pyramid: The league consists of 26 teams organized into three (3) global competitive tiers across two (2) balanced conferences (Conference A and Conference B) and six (6) total divisions:
    • Tier 1 (Premier — 6 Teams): Division 1A (3 teams) and Division 1B (3 teams).
    • Tier 2 (Contenders — 8 Teams): Division 2A (4 teams) and Division 2B (4 teams).
    • Tier 3 (The Grind — 12 Teams): Division 3A (6 teams) and Division 3B (6 teams).
  • Offseason Re-Balancing (Tiers First, Conferences Second): At the conclusion of each offseason's promotion and relegation cycle, all teams are assigned to their respective global tier (Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3) prior to conference placement. Each tier is then divided evenly between Conference A and Conference B via snake-draft seeding based on prior regular-season record (subject to Finals Separation in Section 3.4) to maintain competitive parity across both conferences.

Section 3.2 — Tier 1 Placement & Championship Merit Rules

Tier 1 (6 teams total) is populated from the top down every offseason using the following strict order of priority:

  1. Champion Protection (League Champion Only):
    • Only the League Champion from the preceding 12-team postseason is guaranteed placement in Tier 1 for the following season, regardless of their prior regular-season tier or division finish.
    • The Finals Runner-Up receives no automatic tier promotion or relegation immunity and is placed strictly into whichever tier they earn through regular-season standings.
  2. Tier 1 Retention & Relegation:
    • Under standard conditions, the top two (2) regular-season finishers in Division 1A and Division 1B retain Tier 1 status, while the 3rd-place (last-place) finisher in each division is relegated to Tier 2.
    • If a 3rd-place finisher in Division 1A or Division 1B wins the League Championship, they retain Tier 1 status under Section 3.2(1), and the 2nd-place regular-season finisher in that division is relegated to Tier 2 in their stead.
  3. Tier 2 Incoming Promotion Cap (Guaranteed Minimum 1 Promotion):
    • The two (2) open Tier 1 slots are awarded to the 1st-place regular-season finishers of Division 2A and Division 2B.
    • Because total incoming promotions to Tier 1 are strictly capped at two (2) slots, if a non-Tier-1 team (a non-division-winning Tier 2 team or any Tier 3 team) wins the League Championship, they claim one (1) of those Tier 1 promotion slots via Section 3.2(1)—displacing whichever regular-season division winner between Division 2A and Division 2B holds the inferior overall record. At least one (1) Tier 2 division winner is therefore guaranteed promotion every offseason.

Section 3.3 — Tier 2 & Tier 3 Promotion and Relegation

  1. Tier 3 to Tier 2 Promotion (Division Winners + Wildcard — 3 Teams):
    • Automatic Division Champions (2 Teams): The 1st-place regular-season finisher in Division 3A and the 1st-place regular-season finisher in Division 3B are automatically promoted to Tier 2. (If a 1st-place finisher vaults directly into Tier 1 by winning the League Championship under Section 3.2(1), the 2nd-place regular-season finisher in that division claims the automatic division promotion spot in their stead.)
    • Global Wildcard Promotion (+1 Team): In addition to the two automatic division promotions, the next highest overall regular-season finisher across the remaining Tier 3 teams combined (excluding any team promoted directly to Tier 1 via winning the League Championship) is promoted to Tier 2.
  2. Tier 2 to Tier 3 Relegation (Division Floor + Wildcard — Fixed 3 Teams):
    • Automatic Division Floor (2 Teams): The 4th-place (last-place) regular-season finisher in Division 2A and the 4th-place (last-place) regular-season finisher in Division 2B are automatically relegated to Tier 3. (If a 4th-place finisher wins the League Championship and promotes to Tier 1 under Section 3.2(1), the 3rd-place finisher in that division is relegated in their stead.)
    • Global Wildcard Relegation (+1 Team = 3 Total): In addition to the two automatic division relegations, the next lowest overall regular-season finisher across the remaining Tier 2 teams combined is relegated to Tier 3, maintaining fixed 6 – 8 – 12 tier headcounts every season.

Section 3.4 — Conference Snake-Seeding & Finals Separation

  1. Finals Bracket Separation (FIBA-Style): To prevent an early postseason rematch between the preceding season's finalists:
    • The League Champion is always assigned to Division 1A (Conference A).
    • The Finals Runner-Up is always assigned to Conference B within whichever tier they occupy for the upcoming season (Division 1B, Division 2B, or Division 3B).
  2. Tier 1 Snake-Assignment: Once the League Champion is placed in Division 1A (and the Finals Runner-Up is placed in Division 1B, if in Tier 1), the remaining Tier 1 teams are ranked by prior regular-season record and snake-assigned to fill the remaining slots in Division 1A and Division 1B (so that the top remaining seed joins Division 1B, the next two join Division 1A, and the final seed joins Division 1B).
  3. Tier 2 & Tier 3 Snake-Assignment: Once the final rosters for Tier 2 (8 teams) and Tier 3 (12 teams) are established, each pool is ranked by prior regular-season record and snake-drafted into its respective conference divisions (#1, #4, #5, #8... to Conference A; #2, #3, #6, #7... to Conference B). If the Finals Runner-Up belongs to Tier 2 or Tier 3 and would naturally fall into a Conference A slot via snake order, they swap conference slots with the adjacent seed in their tier so the Runner-Up resides in Conference B.

Section 3.5 — Tiebreakers & Platform Precedence

  • Regular-Season Standings & Cutoff Ties: All ties for division titles, global Tier 3 wildcard promotion cutoffs, global Tier 2 wildcard relegation cutoffs, Tier 1 incoming promotion priority, and offseason snake-draft ordering are resolved in the following sequence:
    1. Overall regular-season winning percentage (Sports.ws official standings);
    2. Total cumulative fantasy points scored across the regular season;
    3. Head-to-head regular-season record between the tied teams;
    4. Superior finish in the preceding 12-team postseason (ranked strictly in descending order: League Champion > Finals Runner-Up > Semifinalists > Quarterfinalists > 1st Round Exits > Non-Playoff Teams).
  • Playoff Bracket & Platform Precedence: The postseason consists of a 12-team single-elimination bracket (6 teams per conference), where the top two (2) division winners in each conference receive first-round byes. In the event of any discrepancy between manual league tables and automated scheduling or bracket seeding on Sports.ws, official Sports.ws platform rules and bracket generation take precedence.

Section 3.6 — Inaugural Pyramid Seeding (End of 2026–27 Season)

At the conclusion of the 2026–27 season, all 26 teams shall be placed into the inaugural 6 – 8 – 12 competitive pyramid based on final 2026–27 postseason finish (ranked strictly as Finals > Semifinals > Quarterfinals > 1st Round > Non-Playoff), with ties within the same elimination round resolved via the regular-season tiebreakers in Section 3.5:

  • Tier 1 (Premier — 6 Teams): League Champion (assigned to Division 1A), Finals Runner-Up (assigned to Division 1B), both Semifinalists (2 teams), and the top two (2) Quarterfinalists by regular-season record.
  • Tier 2 (Contenders — 8 Teams): The remaining two (2) Quarterfinalists, all four (4) 1st Round exits, and the top two (2) non-playoff teams by regular-season record.
  • Tier 3 (The Grind — 12 Teams): The remaining twelve (12) non-playoff teams.

Once each tier pool is established, teams are snake-seeded into Conference A and Conference B divisions in accordance with Section 3.4.

Article IV. Drafts

Draft Lottery

The "3-2-1" Anti-Tanking Model: The draft lottery utilizes a progressive penalty system modeled directly after the 2027 NBA Draft rules. To aggressively discourage intentional losing, the bottom three teams are actively penalized in the lottery probability distribution, while maximum odds are awarded to the middle tier of non-playoff teams.

Lottery Eligibility and Ball Allocation

Assuming a standard 26-team league with 12 playoff teams, fourteen (14) non-playoff teams are eligible for the Draft Lottery. The lottery relies on a total of thirty-three (33) lottery balls, distributed using the 3-2-1 format mapped to the 14 non-playoff teams as follows:

Additional Anti-Tanking Safeguards

  • Slide Limit: The teams with the three worst regular-season records (who receive two lottery balls) can slide no further than the No. 12 overall pick in the draft.
  • Consecutive Pick Restrictions:
    • No team can win the No. 1 overall pick in consecutive drafts.
    • No team can win a top-three pick in three consecutive drafts.
  • Commissioner Override: If a team is caught intentionally losing ("tanking"), the Commissioner retains the absolute power to reduce their lottery odds or forcibly change their draft position.

Keeper Draft

  • Draft Protection: Each team may protect six (6) players from the previous season. If new teams are added, the number of protected players is reduced to 6 − n, where n is the number of new teams.
  • Draft Order Implementation:
    1. Each eligible team is assigned their respective number of lottery balls in a randomized draw.
    2. A live stream of the virtual lottery shall be scheduled and accessible to all league members for verification.
    3. All first-round lottery results are final and binding (subject to the slide limits and consecutive pick rules).
    4. All rounds following the first shall follow reverse standing order from the prior season (independent of the lottery results).
  • Expansion Draft: If a new team is added to the league, an expansion draft shall occur prior to the regular keeper draft. Each existing team may protect 6 − n players, where n is the number of new teams added. Each new team shall draft six (6) players, and may not select players from the rookie class. The selection order for new teams shall follow the current keeper draft order prior to the lottery and shall be determined by a lottery utilizing equal odds for all expansion teams.
  • Dispersal Draft: If a team is disqualified, removed, or otherwise exits the league, a dispersal draft shall occur prior to the protection of players in the regular keeper draft. Teams shall select players in reverse order of prior season standings, with selection order following the current keeper draft order prior to any lottery.

Article V. Regular Season and Schedule

  • Platform Schedule Precedence: The regular-season schedule automatically generated by sports.ws shall govern and be followed as published on the platform. The clauses below describe the intended structure of that generated schedule and do not override an automatically generated sports.ws schedule unless explicitly modified by the Commissioner.
  • The league shall operate on an 82-game season framework, inclusive of regular-season and playoff games.
  • The regular season shall consist of 54 games per team.
  • Each team shall play every other team twice during the regular season, accounting for 50 games (based on the standard 26-team structure).
  • The remaining 4 regular-season matchups shall be randomly assigned by the league platform.
  • The playoffs shall consist of a maximum of 28 games per team, assuming all four rounds go to seven games.
  • The regular season and playoffs shall therefore account for a maximum of 82 games per team.
  • Expansion Scheduling Adjustment: If the league size expands beyond the current 26 teams (up to the strict maximum of 30 teams), the schedule and playoff formats may be adjusted to ensure the 82-game absolute maximum per team is maintained. Any adjustments to the regular season schedule, opponent matchups, or playoff structure due to expansion are subject entirely to the Commissioner's discretion.

Article VI. Playoffs

  • Platform Bracket Precedence: The playoff bracket, seeding, and series schedule automatically generated by sports.ws shall govern as configured on the platform. The clauses below describe the standard playoff format and do not override platform-generated matchups or scheduling unless adjusted by the Commissioner.
  • A total of twelve (12) teams shall qualify for the playoffs, consisting of six (6) teams per conference (Conference A and Conference B).
  • The three division champions in each conference shall automatically qualify for the playoffs.
  • The remaining three (3) playoff spots in each conference shall be awarded as wildcards to the non-division-winning conference teams with the best regular-season records.
  • In each conference, the top two (2) division champions by regular-season record shall occupy the #1 and #2 conference seeds and receive first-round byes, while the third division champion shall occupy the #3 conference seed.
  • The remaining three (3) wildcard teams in each conference shall be seeded #4 through #6 according to their regular-season records.
  • Conference playoff matchups shall follow a 6-team per conference single-elimination bracket:
    Conference Round 1: #3 vs. #6 and #4 vs. #5 (Seeds #1 and #2 receive byes).
  • All playoff rounds shall be best-of-seven series.
  • The winner of each series shall advance to the next round, culminating in the Conference A Champion facing the Conference B Champion in the League Finals.
  • The winner of the championship series shall be declared league champion.
