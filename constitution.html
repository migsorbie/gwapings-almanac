Liga ng mga Gwapings Constitution

Updated: 11/24/2026

Preamble

This document serves as the official Constitution (“the Constitution”) for the keeper league Liga ng mga Gwapings hosted on sports.ws.

Article I. Definitions

  • League – The organized competition governed by this Constitution.
  • Constitution – This document and any amendments officially adopted by the Commissioner.
  • Owner – The individual responsible for managing a team within the League.
  • Team – A franchise, squad, or unit controlled by an Owner and participating in the League.
  • Commissioner – The individual with ultimate authority to interpret, enforce, and amend the rules of the League.

Article II. Policies

Governing Rules

  • Default Platform Rules: The league operates under the default keeper rules of sports.ws, except as modified herein.
  • Superseding Authority: This document supersedes platform rules where conflicts exist, except where platform scheduling or bracket precedence is explicitly stated.
  • Commissioner Authority: The Commissioner retains absolute authority to interpret, amend, or override any rule or decision.
  • Finality of Actions: Any action or inaction by the Commissioner is final and binding.

League Membership

  • Team Approval: All team memberships require prior approval from the Commissioner.
  • Team Exit Notification: Any team planning to exit the league must inform the Commissioner prior to the start of the next season’s draft. Failure to notify in time may result in forfeiture of rights or penalties determined by the Commissioner.
  • New Team Limits & League Cap: A maximum of four (4) new teams may join per season, and total league size shall not exceed thirty (30) teams unless waived by the Commissioner.
  • Replacement Priority: Prospective owners replacing exiting teams take priority over the creation of new expansion teams.

Owner Conduct and Participation

  • Conduct: Trash talk is permitted, but harassment, collusion, or verbally targeting another Owner is prohibited.
  • Disciplinary Action: Owners may submit screenshots of violations to the Commissioner, who may issue warnings or expel an Owner at their discretion.
  • Activity & Trade Responsiveness: Owners are expected to remain active and respond to trade offers within a reasonable timeframe. Patterns of inactivity or obstruction may result in warnings or removal.

Article III. League Structure, Tiers & Movement

Section 3.1 — Tier & Conference Grid

  • Structure (26 Teams): The league is divided into three (3) competitive tiers intersected by two (2) playoff conferences (Conference A and Conference B), forming six (6) divisions:
    • Tier 1 (6 Teams): Division 1A (3 teams, Conference A) and Division 1B (3 teams, Conference B).
    • Tier 2 (8 Teams): Division 2A (4 teams, Conference A) and Division 2B (4 teams, Conference B).
    • Tier 3 (12 Teams): Division 3A (6 teams, Conference A) and Division 3B (6 teams, Conference B).
  • Fluid Re-Balancing: Conferences are non-permanent. Each offseason, teams are assigned to their earned tier first, then re-seeded between Conference A and Conference B under Section 3.4.

Section 3.2 — Tier 1 Placement (6 Teams)

  1. Champion Protection: Only the preceding season's League Champion is guaranteed Tier 1 placement regardless of regular-season finish. The Finals Runner-Up receives no tier protection or relegation immunity.
  2. Retention & Relegation: The top two (2) regular-season finishers in Division 1A and Division 1B retain Tier 1 status; the 3rd-place finisher in each division is relegated to Tier 2. If a 3rd-place finisher wins the League Championship, they remain in Tier 1 and the 2nd-place finisher in that division is relegated instead.
  3. Incoming Promotions (2 Slots): The two (2) open Tier 1 slots go to the 1st-place regular-season finishers of Division 2A and Division 2B. If a non-Tier-1 team wins the League Championship, they claim one (1) of these slots, displacing the Division 2A or 2B winner with the inferior regular-season record.

Section 3.3 — Tier 2 & Tier 3 Movement

  1. Tier 3 to Tier 2 Promotion:
    • Division Winners (2 Teams): The 1st-place regular-season finishers of Division 3A and Division 3B promote to Tier 2. If either wins the League Championship and vaults to Tier 1, the 2nd-place finisher in that division takes the promotion slot.
    • Conditional Wildcard (+1 or +0 Teams): If the League Champion is from Tier 1 or Tier 2, the next highest overall regular-season finisher in Tier 3 also promotes to Tier 2 (3 total). If the League Champion is from Tier 3, no wildcard promotion occurs (2 total).
  2. Tier 2 to Tier 3 Relegation (Fixed 3 Teams):
    • Division Floor (2 Teams): The 4th-place regular-season finishers of Division 2A and Division 2B are relegated to Tier 3. If either wins the League Championship and vaults to Tier 1, the 3rd-place finisher in that division is relegated instead.
    • Global Wildcard (+1 Team): The next lowest overall regular-season finisher across the remaining Tier 2 teams is relegated to Tier 3.

Section 3.4 — Conference Assignment & Finals Separation

  1. Finals Separation: The League Champion is placed in Conference A (Division 1A) first, and the Finals Runner-Up (ranked #1 in their tier pool if in Tier 2 or Tier 3) is placed in Conference B of their earned tier (Division 1B, Division 2B, or Division 3B) first.
  2. Snake-Seeding: All other teams in each tier are ranked by prior regular-season record and assigned via snake draft (#1, #4, #5, #8... to Conference A; #2, #3, #6, #7... to Conference B), skipping any team already assigned.

Section 3.5 — Tiebreakers & Platform Precedence

  • Tiebreaker Order: All ties for division titles, promotion/relegation cutoffs, and snake-seeding order are broken by:
    1. Overall regular-season winning percentage;
    2. Total cumulative regular-season fantasy points;
    3. Head-to-head regular-season record;
    4. Preceding postseason finish (League Champion > Finals Runner-Up > Semifinalists > Quarterfinalists > 1st Round Exits > Non-Playoff Teams).
  • Platform Precedence: Official sports.ws standings, scheduling, and bracket generation govern in the event of any discrepancy with manual tables.

Section 3.6 — Inaugural Seeding (End of 2026–27 Season)

At the end of the 2026–27 season, teams are placed into the inaugural 6 – 8 – 12 tiers by 2026–27 postseason finish (ties within the same round broken via Section 3.5), then seeded into conferences under Section 3.4:

  • Tier 1 (6 Teams): League Champion, Finals Runner-Up, both Semifinalists (2), and the top two (2) Quarterfinalists.
  • Tier 2 (8 Teams): Remaining two (2) Quarterfinalists, all four (4) 1st Round exits, and the top two (2) non-playoff teams.
  • Tier 3 (12 Teams): Remaining twelve (12) non-playoff teams.

Section 3.7 — Replacement, Contraction & Expansion

  • Replacement Ownership: A new Owner taking over an existing franchise inherits its earned tier and division assignment.
  • Contraction (Unreplaced Exit): If a franchise exits without replacement, teams below its tier shift up one slot by regular-season merit so Tier 1 (6) and Tier 2 (8) remain full, with Tier 3 absorbing the vacancy.
  • Expansion (27 to 30 Teams): Expansion franchises enter in Tier 3, split evenly between Division 3A and Division 3B via snake-seeding, while Tier 1 (6) and Tier 2 (8) remain fixed.

  • Consecutive Pick Restrictions: No team may win the No. 1 overall pick in consecutive drafts, nor win a top-three pick in three consecutive drafts.
  • Anti-Tanking Override: The Commissioner may reduce lottery odds or alter draft positions for intentional losing.

Keeper Draft

  • Keepers: Each team may protect six (6) players from the prior season, reduced to 6 − n when n expansion teams are added.
  • Draft Order: Round 1 follows the live-streamed lottery draw (subject to slide limits and consecutive pick rules). All subsequent rounds follow reverse prior-season standings.
  • Expansion Draft: Held prior to the keeper draft when new teams join. Expansion teams draft six (6) non-rookie players in an equal-odds lottery order before the main lottery.
  • Dispersal Draft: Held prior to keeper protections if a team exits the league, with teams selecting in reverse prior-season standing order.

Article V. Regular Season and Schedule

  • Platform Schedule Precedence: The regular-season schedule generated by sports.ws governs as published on the platform.
  • Season Length: Within an 82-game framework, the regular season consists of 56 games per team (82 minus the 26 maximum possible playoff games).
  • Schedule Generation: For 26 teams, sports.ws runs a double round-robin against all 25 opponents (50 games) and assigns the remaining 6 games by cycling back through each team's opening round-robin opponents.

Article VI. Playoffs

  • Platform Bracket Precedence: The playoff bracket, seeding, and schedule generated by sports.ws govern as configured on the platform.
  • Qualification & Seeding (12 Teams): Six (6) teams qualify per conference—the three (3) division champions plus three (3) wildcards with the best remaining conference records. The top two (2) division champions per conference take Seeds #1 and #2 (receiving first-round byes), the third division champion takes Seed #3, and the wildcards take Seeds #4 through #6.
  • Bracket Matchups (Per Conference):
    Round 1: #3 vs. #6 and #4 vs. #5 (Seeds #1 and #2 receive byes).
    Round 2: #1 vs. lowest remaining seed and #2 vs. highest remaining seed.
  • Series Format: Round 1 is a best-of-five (5) series. All subsequent rounds (Conference Semifinals, Conference Finals, and League Finals) are best-of-seven (7) series.
